Privacy Policy

BlueGreen Technology Group — Internal Accounting Integration
Effective date: August 13, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers a private, internal software integration ("the Application") operated by BlueGreen Technology Group ("we," "us"). The Application connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's authorized APIs solely to access the accounting data of BlueGreen Technology Group's own QuickBooks Online company file(s). It is not distributed, sold, or made available to any third party, and it has no external users.

2. Data we access

The Application accesses accounting records belonging to BlueGreen Technology Group, including the chart of accounts, transactions, vendors, and financial reports, using OAuth 2.0 authorization granted by our own account administrator. It does not access data belonging to any other person or business.

3. How data is used

Data is used exclusively for BlueGreen Technology Group's internal bookkeeping, reconciliation, and financial analysis. Portions of accounting data may be processed by an AI assistant service (Anthropic's Claude) at our direction and under our supervision as part of this internal workflow, subject to that provider's commercial data-handling terms. Data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to any other third party.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in a protected configuration file on a single company-controlled machine with disk encryption. Access is limited to authorized personnel of BlueGreen Technology Group. The Application requests read-only access by default; write operations are disabled at the application level except when deliberately enabled for supervised bookkeeping entries.

5. Data retention and deletion

The Application does not maintain its own database of QuickBooks data. Accounting data remains in QuickBooks Online. Local working files derived from it are retained only as long as needed for the bookkeeping task and are deleted thereafter. Disconnecting the Application from QuickBooks Online revokes its access immediately.
